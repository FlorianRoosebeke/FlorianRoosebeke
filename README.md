## About me

I am currently studying at [Holberton School](https://www.holbertonschool.fr/) to prepare for the French professional title **Concepteur Développeur d'Applications** (RNCP).

Alongside my studies, I am working as a **Data & Digitalisation work-study student at Verbus**, where I work on projects involving software development, data and internal digital tools.

I enjoy building things, figuring out how they work, and finding simple solutions to practical problems. Before switching to tech, I spent more than 10 years in the social sector. That experience taught me to adapt, analyse situations and work with different people — skills that I now use in a completely different environment.

I am still learning, and that's one of the things I enjoy most about this career change.

## What I'm working on

- Building **Verbus 360**, a web application made with Laravel and Vue.js
- Developing and improving internal tools at Verbus
- Working with data and building useful ways to analyse it
- Looking for ways to simplify and automate repetitive processes
- Using AI tools to speed up development and learn faster
- Continuing to improve my skills through real-world projects and my studies
